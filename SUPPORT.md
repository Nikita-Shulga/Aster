# Getting help

**Something doesn't work?** First check the [FAQ in the README](README.md#faq) and, on a Mac, [docs/MAC.md](docs/MAC.md) (Full Disk Access, first launch, login items permission).

**Still stuck?** [Open a bug report](https://github.com/Nikita-Shulga/Aster/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Say which page you were on, what you clicked, what happened and what you expected. Your Aster version is in **Settings → About**.

**Want to undo something?** Open **History**: registry fixes, edits and startup removals can be restored with one click. Files Aster removed from the Disk analyzer, Duplicates or leftover scans are in your Recycle Bin / Trash.

Aster keeps its settings, history and registry backups here:

- Windows: `%APPDATA%\Aster` (backups in `backups\`)
- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Aster`

**Have an idea?** [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/Nikita-Shulga/Aster/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

**Found a security problem?** Don't open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Aster is maintained in spare time, so replies can take a few days.
